about me

I like the part of software most people skip: what happens after the code works. How it gets packaged, how it ships, how it stays up, and how someone might get in.

Right now I'm in my fourth year of a B.E. in Software Engineering at CST, Royal University of Bhutan. In 2024 I interned at GovTech Bhutan's Department of Software Development, testing systems that were still being built and reporting the defects and edge cases to the developers.

Outside class you'll find me doing PortSwigger labs, playing CTFs, or turning a class project into something that actually deploys.
