About me

I'm a fourth-year B.E. Software Engineering student at the College of Science and Technology, Royal University of Bhutan, majoring in Software Security.

Most of my time goes into the space where development meets operations and security: building full-stack apps, packaging them into containers, automating how they ship, and then testing them the way an attacker would. At GovTech Bhutan I tested systems still in development and reported the defects and edge cases to the developers.

Outside class I do PortSwigger labs and CTFs. I was also on the team that won the 2024 Innovation Bootcamp.
