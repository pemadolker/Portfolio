Hi, I'm Pema.

I'm in my fourth year of a B.E. in Software Engineering at the College of Science and Technology, Royal University of Bhutan, majoring in Software Security.

I've never stuck to just the front end or the back end. Over four years I've built web apps, packaged them into containers, deployed them through CI/CD pipelines, run them on Kubernetes, set up AWS services, wired up IoT sensors, and played CTFs. I like knowing how all the pieces connect, and where they can fail.
